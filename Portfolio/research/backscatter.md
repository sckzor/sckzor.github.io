# Wirelessly Powered Sensors

### August 2024 - May 2025

![Wireless Sensor](/Images/portfolio/wpt-1400.webp)


As part of a research project at school I developed an extremely low power wireless sensing platform that runs off of scavenged RF power. The current version of the sensor is able to continuously run off of less than a tenth of a milliwatt of power at 3.3 GHz. It relays data it gathers from an enviornmental sensor by backscattering (reflecting or harvesting) the signal that energizes it.

I wrote a conference paper which I presented at the [IEEE's Topical Conference on Antennas and Propagation for Wireless Communications](https://ieeexplore.ieee.org/xpl/conhome/11190314/proceeding).  Copies of the research paper including design of the sensor and test results are available on [IEEE Xplore](https://ieeexplore.ieee.org/document/11190446/).

This work was undertaken as part of an [independent study + research (ISR)](https://www.olin.edu/academics-registrars-office/independent-study-research) project at Olin College of Engineering.
