Server Rack

July 2023

I purchased a group of five servers extremely cheaply for an e-waste sale. I wanted a good way to store them so I built this rack out of modular aluminum bars that I got from the frame of an old piece of automotive test equipment one of my dad's friends was getting rid of. It looks much nicer than the previous pile of equipment on the ground, is able to be easily moved around and gives me a nice surface to work on when I am managing my servers. All the servers are networked together and run ProxMox. They are used to host my personal games servers as well as run compute jobs when necessary.
