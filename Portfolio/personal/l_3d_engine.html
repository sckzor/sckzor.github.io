Car Cooling Controller

May 2021 - June 2022

My cousin has a Foxbody Mustang with a mechanical cooling system. In order to improve performance, he opted to retro-fit the vehicle with an electric cooling system. I built this device for him to control the cooling system. The device reads various engine sensors and controls the radiator fans based on the available data. All the performace parameters are configurable in software using the knobs and button.
