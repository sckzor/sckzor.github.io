# Thrust Test Data Logger

### December 2024

![Test Stand](/Images/portfolio/test_stand-1400.webp)

This board was developed to ignite a solid rocket motors and record high frequency thrust and pressure data from the motor. It is used as part of Olin College of Engineering’s rocketry team’s motor test stand.
