Patch Antenna Optimizer

April 2025 - May 2025

I co-developed a planar patch antenna optimizer using a custom-built three dimensional method of moments solver to calcuate the radiation pattern and gain of an arbitrarily sized patch antenna. The implementation is written in MATLAB.

I manufactured the antenna that was deemed desirable by the solver to check its parameters when realized. The solver was pretty good overall. The center frequency was off by ~20%, however given the poor manufacturing tollerance I held while building the antenna, I was impressed it was close.
